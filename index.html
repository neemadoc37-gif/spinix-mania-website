M-PESA PAYMENT DETAILS

Paybill information

4043233
Account number format

Customer phone number

Use the phone number registered to the customer's Spinix account as the account reference, once the official payment flow is enabled.

How to pay (when authorised)

  1. Open M-Pesa on your phone.
  2. Select Lipa na M-Pesa.
  3. Select Pay Bill.
  4. Enter Business Number 4043233.
  5. Enter your registered phone number as Account Number.
  6. Enter only the amount shown in the approved checkout.
  7. Confirm the recipient and amount before entering your PIN.
Payment status: This project does not yet verify Paybill C2B payments or confirm orders automatically. Do not treat an M-Pesa message, screenshot or payment reference as proof of a completed Spinix order. Automated confirmation requires a production Daraja callback, secure server-side verification and live testing. Paid prize participation must remain disabled until the required Kenyan regulatory approvals are obtained.
